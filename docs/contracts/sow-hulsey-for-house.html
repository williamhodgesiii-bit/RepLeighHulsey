3. Scope of Services

3.1 Design and Build

Provider will design and build a custom, mobile‑first website of six (6) pages, itemized in Exhibit A: Home, About, On the Issues, News, Contact, and Donate. The build includes:

  • A custom visual design derived from the Beneficiary's existing logo and color palette. No third‑party theme or page‑builder template will be resold as original design work.
  • Responsive layouts verified on current versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox, on both mobile and desktop viewports.
  • Semantic markup, descriptive alternative text on images supplied with captions, keyboard‑operable navigation, and visible focus states.
  • Page titles, meta descriptions, social sharing preview tags, a sitemap, and a robots file.
  • Performance work appropriate to a static site: compressed and correctly sized images, deferred non‑critical assets, and no render‑blocking third‑party scripts added by Provider.

3.2 Third‑Party Connections

Provider will connect the Website to Third‑Party Services designated by Client, including a donation platform (for example WinRed or Anedot), an email‑list or contact‑form endpoint, and social media profiles. Connection means linking to, embedding, or posting to the interface those services publish.

Provider does not build, host or operate payment processing. Donations are processed entirely by Client's chosen donation platform under Client's own account and agreement with that platform. Provider never takes custody of contributor funds or payment card data, and is not a processor, vendor or agent of that platform.

3.3 Self‑Service Publishing

Provider will configure the Website so that Client can create, edit and publish news and blog posts without Provider's involvement, and will supply written instructions and one (1) live walkthrough of up to sixty (60) minutes. Client is solely responsible for the content it publishes through this facility.

3.4 Hosting, Deployment and DNS

Provider will host the Website on Vercel within a deployment environment Provider controls, and will configure DNS so that Client's domain resolves to it, including issuance and renewal of a TLS certificate. Provider retains control of the hosting stack, source repository and build pipeline for the duration of the Maintenance Term, which is what allows Provider to support the Website at the rate set out in Section 9.

3.5 Ongoing Maintenance

From Launch, and for so long as the monthly fee is paid, Provider will provide:

  • Hosting, TLS certificate renewal, DNS record management, and uptime monitoring.
  • Correction of defects — failures of the Website to work as built — at no additional charge.
  • Security and dependency updates, and off‑site backups of the source repository.
  • Up to sixty (60) minutes per calendar month of small content edits: swapping or adding text, replacing or adding images, updating links, and similar changes that do not alter layout or add functionality. Unused minutes do not carry forward.
  • Response to support requests within one (1) Business Day, and to a total outage of the Website within four (4) hours of Provider becoming aware of it.

Work beyond these inclusions is billed under Section 9.4 or by Change Order.

7. Revisions

The fee in Section 9.1 includes two (2) rounds of revisions at the design stage (Milestone 4) and one (1) round after the full build is presented (Milestone 6). A “round” is one consolidated set of written feedback delivered by Client's designated contact.

A revision means adjustment within the approved direction — copy, imagery, color, spacing, ordering of sections. A change of creative direction after design approval, the addition of pages or features, or a restructuring of the approved layout is not a revision and requires a Change Order. Further rounds beyond those included are billed at the rate in Section 9.4.

8. Acceptance

On presentation of the completed build, Client shall have seven (7) Business Days to test the Website and either accept it or deliver a single written list of specific, reproducible failures to conform to this SOW. Provider will correct conforming failures and re‑present, beginning a new five (5) Business Day review limited to the items previously raised.

Deemed acceptance. The Website is deemed accepted on the earliest of: (a) Client's written acceptance; (b) expiry of a review period without a written list of failures; or (c) Client's use of the Website in public, including any publication of its address to voters or supporters. Matters of taste, or content Client itself supplied, are not failures to conform.

10. Term and Termination

10.1 Term. This SOW begins on the Effective Date and continues through Launch, and thereafter for successive one‑month periods for so long as the monthly fee is paid (the “Maintenance Term”).

10.2 Termination during the build. Either Party may terminate on fifteen (15) days' written notice. On termination, Client shall pay for all work performed to the date of termination, and the deposit is retained. If Client terminates after design approval but before Launch, Client shall pay seventy‑five percent (75%) of the one‑time fee, less amounts already paid.

10.3 Termination of maintenance. After Launch, either Party may terminate the Maintenance Term on thirty (30) days' written notice, effective at the end of the then‑current paid month. Monthly fees already paid are not refundable.

10.4 Termination for cause. Either Party may terminate immediately if the other materially breaches and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days of written notice. Provider may terminate immediately if Client uses the Website for anything unlawful.

10.5 Effect. On any termination, and provided all sums due have been paid, Provider will within thirty (30) days deliver an export of the Website source and content in a standard format, and cooperate reasonably in transferring the domain's DNS to a destination Client nominates. Provider is not obliged to continue hosting past the effective date of termination. Sections 11 through 19 survive.
