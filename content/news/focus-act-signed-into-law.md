Governor Kay Ivey has signed the FOCUS Act into law. The legislation, House Bill 166, was sponsored by State Representative Leigh Hulsey and prohibits student cell phone use in Alabama public schools during the school day.

FOCUS stands for Freeing Our Classrooms of Unnecessary Screens for Safety.

## Provisions of the Law

Under the FOCUS Act, students in Alabama public elementary and secondary schools may not possess or use a wireless communication device during the school day unless the device is powered off and stored away from the student.

The law provides exceptions for:

- Emergency situations
- Students with an Individualized Education Plan or a Section 504 plan
- Instances in which a device is necessary for instructional purposes

The legislation additionally requires local boards of education to adopt an internet safety policy governing school devices, and requires students to complete a social media safety course before entering the eighth grade.

## Passage

The bill passed the Alabama House of Representatives by a vote of 79 to 15 and the Alabama Senate by a vote of 30 to 2 before being sent to the Governor.

"As a parent, equipping my children to excel in all aspects of life is priority number one, and what they learn in the classroom plays an immense role in that," Hulsey said. She has said the law will limit distractions and give every student a better opportunity to get the most out of the school day.
