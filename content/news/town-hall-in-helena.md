State Representative Leigh Hulsey will host an open town hall for residents of House District 15 on **Saturday, September 27**, from 10:00 a.m. to noon at Helena City Hall.

The meeting is free and open to anyone who lives in the district. No registration is required.

## What to expect

Rep. Hulsey will open with a short update on the 2026 legislative session, then take questions from the floor for the remainder of the meeting. Topics she expects to cover include:

- How schools across the district are implementing the FOCUS Act
- Road and infrastructure funding for Helena, McCalla and western Hoover
- The growth pressures facing Shelby and Jefferson counties
- House Bill 399 and the new limits on data center tax abatements

> "The best part of this job is hearing directly from the people I work for. Bring your questions — I would rather have a hard one than a quiet room."

## Details

**When:** Saturday, September 27, 10:00 a.m. to noon
**Where:** Helena City Hall, 816 Highway 52 East, Helena, AL 35080
**Parking:** Free in the lot behind the building

Residents who cannot attend can still [send a question through the contact page](contact.html), and the campaign will follow up directly.
