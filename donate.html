Where it goes

No consultants in Washington. Signs in Helena.

This is a state House district. The budget is small, and every line of it is visible from your driveway.

  • $25 — a yard sign in a neighbor's yard.
  • $50 — literature for a Saturday of door knocking.
  • $100 — a mail piece to a full precinct.
  • Monthly — the most useful thing you can do. Predictable money is what lets a campaign plan more than two weeks out.
Grant funding, a school phone law, and a phone that gets answered. That's three years. Help make it seven.

Can't give right now? Take a yard sign instead.
