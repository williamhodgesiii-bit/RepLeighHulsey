Where It Goes

A state House race is a local race. The budget is modest and it is spent almost entirely on reaching voters in Helena, McCalla, Bessemer and western Hoover.

  • $25 covers a yard sign.
  • $50 covers literature for a Saturday of door knocking.
  • $100 covers a mail piece to a precinct.
  • Monthly giving is the most useful support a campaign can receive, because it allows the campaign to plan ahead.

If you would rather help in another way, the campaign always needs volunteers and yard sign locations. Let us know.

Leigh Hulsey is a Republican serving House District 15 in the Alabama House of Representatives.
