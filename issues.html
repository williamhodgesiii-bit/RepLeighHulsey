On the Issues

Where Leigh stands, and what she has worked on for House District 15.

Education

Putting Students and Teachers First

Leigh sponsored House Bill 166, known as the FOCUS Act, which prohibits student cell phone use in Alabama public schools during the school day. Under the law, devices must be powered off and stored away from the student.

The law provides exceptions for emergencies, for students with an Individualized Education Plan or a Section 504 plan, and for instances in which a device is necessary for instruction. It also requires local boards of education to adopt an internet safety policy and requires students to complete a social media safety course before entering the eighth grade.

The bill passed the House 79 to 15 and the Senate 30 to 2, and was signed into law by Governor Kay Ivey in May 2025. Leigh continues to support policies that give teachers the authority and the resources to run their classrooms.

Economy

Jobs and Small Business

Leigh owns and operates CrossFit Alabaster and previously served as president of the Alabaster Business Alliance. That experience informs how she evaluates tax and regulatory policy in Montgomery.

In the 2026 session she sponsored House Bill 399, which reduces the maximum tax abatement period for data processing centers from 30 years to 20 unless a project meets specified community investment thresholds. The bill also restricts abatement of the state's four percent sales and use tax for facilities with a peak demand of 100 megawatts or greater. It passed the Senate unanimously.

Alabama continues to compete for major projects and the jobs they bring. Leigh believes incentive agreements should be structured so that the communities hosting those projects share in the benefit.

Public Safety

Supporting Law Enforcement and First Responders

House District 15 is one of the fastest-growing areas in Alabama. Population growth places steady pressure on police, fire and emergency medical services, and local budgets do not always keep pace.

Leigh has supported legislation strengthening public safety and works directly with local departments on equipment needs and grant applications. Departments and agencies in the district that have a funding need are encouraged to contact the office.

Infrastructure

Roads and Growth

Growth in Helena, McCalla, Bessemer and western Hoover has outpaced the road network in parts of the district. Infrastructure projects move through a long planning and funding process, and districts advance in that process when someone is consistently advocating for them.

Leigh worked on the planning and funding of road projects during her service on the Helena City Council. In the Legislature she has continued to press for transportation investment in District 15, and has helped secure more than $360,000 in grant funding for community needs and local projects.

Values

Life, Faith and Family

Leigh is pro-life and has made protecting unborn life a priority since her first campaign for office. She is a conservative Christian and a member of Church of the Highlands.

She is a supporter of parental rights in education and of the Second Amendment, and has consistently opposed expansions of government authority into decisions she believes belong to families.

Service

Veterans and Military Families

Supporting active-duty service members, military dependents and veterans has been among Leigh's stated priorities since she first ran for the Legislature.

That includes professional licensing that transfers between states, school enrollment policies that do not penalize students whose families relocate, and a district office that responds when a veteran needs help navigating a state agency.
