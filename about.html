About Leigh

State Representative, Alabama House District 15

Leigh Hulsey has lived in Shelby County her entire life. She grew up in Pelham and graduated from Pelham High School before attending Auburn University, where she earned a degree in human development and family studies.

Leigh and her husband Dennis have been married for more than 27 years. They raised three children in Shelby County, all of whom are now grown. Leigh spent twelve years at home with her family before returning to the workforce as a paralegal.

She later opened her own small business, CrossFit Alabaster, which she continues to own and operate. Through that work she became active in the local business community and served as president of the Alabaster Business Alliance.

Public Service

Leigh's service in elected office began on the Helena City Council. Her work there centered on municipal budgets, economic development, and the planning and funding of road projects, which are the day-to-day responsibilities of a growing city.

In November 2022, voters in House District 15 elected Leigh to the Alabama House of Representatives. She was sworn in on November 9, 2022. Two months later, House Republicans elected her to serve as the caucus freshman representative, a position that gave first-term members a voice in leadership discussions.

In the Legislature

Leigh has sponsored two bills that have been signed into law. The FOCUS Act, passed in 2025, prohibits student cell phone use in Alabama public schools during the school day. House Bill 399, enacted in 2026, limits the length of tax abatements available to large data processing centers.

Working with local officials, she has also helped bring more than $360,000 in grant funding back to House District 15 for community needs and local projects.

The citizens of District 15 deserve a God-fearing, qualified, conservative, pro-jobs leader fighting for them.

Leigh Hulsey

Faith and Community

Leigh is a member of Church of the Highlands. She serves on the Shelby County Republican Executive Committee and as vice president of campaigns for the Republican Women of North Shelby County.
