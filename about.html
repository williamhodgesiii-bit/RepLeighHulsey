Her story

Shelby County, start to finish.

Leigh grew up in Pelham and graduated from Pelham High School. She went to Auburn and studied human development and family studies, which is a formal way of saying she studied how families actually work. She has lived in Shelby County her entire life.

Then she did what a lot of women in this district did: she stayed home for twelve years and raised three kids. When the youngest got old enough, she went back to work as a paralegal for a few years. And then, at an age when most people decide it's too late to start something, she opened CrossFit Alabaster.

Owning a small business in Alabama teaches you things a policy briefing can't. Payroll on the fifteenth. Insurance renewals. The specific feeling of reading a new regulation and doing the math on what it costs you. That's the background she brought to the Alabaster Business Alliance, where she served as president, and it's the background she brought to the Helena City Council.

The citizens of District 15 deserve a God-fearing, qualified, conservative, pro-jobs leader fighting for them. Leigh Hulsey

On the council she worked municipal budgets, economic development, and the planning and funding of road projects — the least glamorous and most consequential work in local government. In 2022, District 15 sent her to Montgomery. Her colleagues elected her the House Republican Caucus's freshman representative the following January.

Three years in, the pattern hasn't changed. She's still at the gym. She's still at the ballfields. She still answers her phone.

The path

How she got here

  1. Growing up

    Pelham, Alabama

    Pelham High School, then Auburn University — human development and family studies. A lifelong Shelby County resident.

  2. Twelve years

    At home with three kids

    Married to Dennis for more than 27 years. Three grown children, all raised here.

  3. Then

    Paralegal, then business owner

    A few years in a law office, then she opened CrossFit Alabaster — and later served as president of the Alabaster Business Alliance.

  1. Local government

    Helena City Council

    Municipal budgets, economic development, and planning and funding road projects.

  2. November 2022

    Alabama House, District 15

    Sworn in November 9, 2022, representing parts of Helena, McCalla, Bessemer and western Hoover.

  3. January 2023

    House GOP leadership

    Elected by her colleagues as the caucus's freshman representative.

  4. 2025 & 2026

    Two bills signed into law

    The FOCUS Act on school cell phones, and HB399 reining in long-term data center tax abatements.

Outside the State House

Still coaching. Still at the ballfields.

Leigh serves on the Shelby County GOP executive committee and as vice president of campaigns for the Republican Women of North Shelby County. She's a member of Church of the Highlands. And on most weekday mornings she is where she has been for years — at the gym, before the sun comes up.
